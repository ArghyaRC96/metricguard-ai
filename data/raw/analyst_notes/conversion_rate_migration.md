# Conversion Rate Migration Review

author: Arjun Kapoor
team: Growth Analytics
date: 2026-06-10
related_metric: conversion_rate

Conversion Rate v2 was deployed to the Growth & Marketing Dashboard.

The new metric focuses specifically on checkout-funnel efficiency:

Completed Checkouts / Checkout Starts

Historical reporting using v1 measured purchasing customers relative to total
site sessions.

Values from v1 and v2 should not be compared as if they represent the same
business concept.

The June change in reported Conversion Rate was expected.
