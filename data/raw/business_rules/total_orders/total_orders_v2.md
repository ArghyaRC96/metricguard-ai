## Definition

Total Orders includes orders with a successful payment.

## Business Context

The enterprise KPI definition was revised to align order reporting with
financially recognized customer transactions.

Operational teams may continue tracking placed orders separately, but those
counts should not be presented as the enterprise Total Orders KPI.
