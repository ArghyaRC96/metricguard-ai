## Definition

Net Revenue is calculated as:

Gross Revenue - Discounts - Completed Refunds

## Business Context

Northstar revised Net Revenue to treat customer discounts as a direct
reduction of revenue.

Chargebacks were still reported separately and were not deducted from
Net Revenue.
