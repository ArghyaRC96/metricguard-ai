## Definition

Net Revenue is calculated as:

Gross Revenue - Discounts - Completed Refunds - Chargebacks

## Business Context

Beginning April 1, 2026, Northstar Commerce updated the official Net Revenue
definition to include posted chargebacks.

All reporting assets using Net Revenue should migrate to Version 3 for periods
on or after the effective date.
