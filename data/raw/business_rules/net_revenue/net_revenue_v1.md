## Definition

Net Revenue is calculated as:

Gross Revenue - Completed Refunds

## Business Context

This was Northstar Commerce's original Net Revenue definition.

Discounts were treated separately for reporting purposes and were not deducted
from Net Revenue.

Chargebacks were not yet incorporated into the official metric definition.
