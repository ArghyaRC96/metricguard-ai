# INC-003 — Operations and Finance Total Orders Discrepancy

incident_id: INC-003
status: closed
severity: low
opened_date: 2026-01-09
closed_date: 2026-01-13
owner: Data Analytics
affected_metric: total_orders
affected_assets:
  - Operations Dashboard
  - Finance Revenue Dashboard

## Summary

Operations consistently reports more Total Orders than Finance.

## Investigation

Operations counts all valid placed orders except cancelled orders.

Finance uses the enterprise Total Orders definition introduced on
December 1, 2025, which counts successfully paid orders.

Pending, returned, or otherwise unpaid operational orders may therefore appear
in Operations reporting but not in the Finance KPI.

## Resolution

No underlying data defect was identified.

The two dashboards serve different analytical purposes.

Operations may continue using placed-order logic, but the metric should be
clearly labeled so it is not confused with the enterprise Total Orders KPI.

## Classification

Expected semantic difference — not a data pipeline failure.
