# INC-004 — Conversion Rate Shift After Metric Migration

incident_id: INC-004
status: resolved

closed_date: 2026-06-10
owner: Growth Analytics
affected_metric: conversion_rate

## Summary

Conversion Rate changed materially after the June reporting release.

## Investigation

The enterprise Conversion Rate definition changed on June 1, 2026.

The previous definition measured:

Distinct purchasing customers / Total sessions

The new definition measures:

Completed checkouts / Checkout starts

The Growth & Marketing Dashboard was intentionally migrated to Version 2.

## Resolution

The change was confirmed as expected behavior resulting from the metric
definition migration.

No data defect was identified.
