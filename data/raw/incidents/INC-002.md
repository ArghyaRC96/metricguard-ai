# INC-002 — Active Customer Count Differs Across Teams

incident_id: INC-002
status: open
severity: medium
opened_date: 2026-03-12
owner: Customer Analytics
affected_metric: active_customers
affected_assets:
  - Growth & Marketing Dashboard

## Summary

Growth Analytics reports a materially higher Active Customer count than the
current enterprise customer KPI.

## Observations

The Growth & Marketing Dashboard counts identified customers with recent
website or mobile activity.

The current enterprise definition introduced on March 1, 2026 requires at
least one successfully paid order during the previous 30 days.

## Current Assessment

The Growth dashboard is still using the previous engagement-based definition.

## Required Action

Review whether the Growth dashboard should migrate to Active Customers v2 or
rename its existing metric to Digital Active Customers.
