# INC-001 — Executive and Finance Net Revenue Mismatch

incident_id: INC-001
status: open
severity: high
opened_date: 2026-04-08
owner: BI Platform
affected_metric: net_revenue
affected_assets:
  - Executive KPI Dashboard
  - Finance Revenue Dashboard

## Summary

Finance and Executive dashboards began reporting different Net Revenue values
for reporting periods after April 1, 2026.

## Observations

Finance Analytics confirmed that the Finance Revenue Dashboard was migrated to
Net Revenue Version 3.

The Executive KPI Dashboard still appears to calculate Net Revenue without
deducting posted chargebacks.

## Current Assessment

The discrepancy is believed to be caused by a metric-version mismatch.

Finance uses Net Revenue v3.

Executive reporting still appears to use Net Revenue v2.

## Required Action

Review the Executive KPI mart and dashboard configuration and migrate them to
the current enterprise Net Revenue definition.
